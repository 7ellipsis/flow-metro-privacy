File Sense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Developer: 7ellipsis.

What File Sense accesses

With your permission (Android "All files access"), File Sense reads and changes files in your device storage so you can browse and manage them. This happens on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Cloud drives: only when you sign in to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, File Sense talks directly to that provider to list, download and upload the files you choose. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your device.
  • Share over Wi-Fi / FTP server: only while you turn it on, other devices on your local network can reach the folder you share, protected by a one-time token or password.

File Sense has no ads, no analytics and no account. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled by Google Play; we receive only the purchase confirmation.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on the File Sense Google Play page.
