Cal Sense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Developer: 7ellipsis.

Calendar

With your permission Cal Sense reads and writes events in your device's calendars. Events never leave your device through Cal Sense; any sync is done by your calendar account provider.

Weather

If you allow approximate location, Cal Sense sends coordinates rounded to two decimal places to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. Without location it uses an IP-based or default city.

Reminders

Reminder alarms are scheduled on your device.

No ads, no analytics, no account. Optional in-app purchases are handled by Google Play.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on the Cal Sense Google Play page.
