Flow Phone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. Developer: 7ellipsis.

Calls and call log

As your default phone app, Flow Phone places and receives calls through Android and shows your call log. Call history stays on your device and is never uploaded by Flow Phone.

Contacts

Flow Phone reads your contacts to show names and photos, and adds or edits them only when you ask. A contact you save goes to the account you pick (for example your Google account, which syncs it as it does for any app); Flow Phone itself never uploads your contacts.

Camera

Only when you tap video during a call, Flow Phone asks for the camera and the front camera feeds your carrier's video call. Nothing is recorded or stored.

No ads, no analytics, no account. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
