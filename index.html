Privacy Policy

App: Flow Metro – Tile Launcher
Developer: 7ellipsis
Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Flow Metro is a Windows Phone-style home screen launcher for Android. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All processing happens entirely on your device.

Permissions and their purpose

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE) – Used solely to draw WP-style navigation buttons over other apps, display the volume bar when hardware volume keys are pressed, and open Action Center notifications via the global edge gesture. The service may read on-screen accessibility labels only to support these features. No personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE) – A persistent foreground service keeps the overlay UI (navigation tiles, volume bar, action center) running while the launcher is active. This service performs no network activity.
  • Read Media Images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) – Lets you choose photos from your gallery as tile backgrounds or home screen wallpapers. Images are read locally and never uploaded.
  • Read Media Video (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO) – Lets you set a local video file as a live wallpaper. Videos are read locally and never uploaded.
  • Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) – Used to list installed apps so they can be pinned as live tiles on the home screen. The app list is stored locally and never transmitted.

Data collection

Flow Metro collects no personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting service, and no account system. The app works fully offline.

Third-party services

Flow Metro does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
