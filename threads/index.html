Flow Threads — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Developer: 7ellipsis.

Messages

As your default SMS app, Flow Threads reads, sends and receives SMS and MMS through Android's messaging system. Messages are stored on your device by Android and are never uploaded by Flow Threads.

Contacts

Flow Threads reads your contacts to show names and photos and to pick recipients. Contacts never leave your device.

No ads, no analytics, no account. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Contact

Use the developer email shown on the Flow Threads Google Play page.
